{{Qisqa tavsif|Suriya tug‘ilgan yamanlik adib va astronom}}
'''Amīn al-Dīn Abu ʾl-Ghanāʾim Muslim ibn Maḥmūd al-Shayzarī'''<ref>{{harvnb|Sellheim|1997}} give his full name as Amīn al-Dīn Abu ʾl-Ghan̄aʾim Muslim ibn Abi ʾl-Thanāʾ Maḥmūd ibn Sanad al-Dawla Jamāl al-Mulk Abi ʾl-Faḍāʾil Niʿma ibn Sanad al-Dawla Abi ʾl-ʿAṭāʾ Arslān (Raslān) ibn Yaḥyā. {{harvnb|Brockelmann|2017|loc=vol. 1, p. 262}}, gives his ''[[taxallus]]'' as Amīn al-Dawla.</ref> ({{Floruit}} 1201–1225) Suriya tug‘ilgan yamanlik ''[[adib]]'' va astronom edi.

Al-Shayzarī [[Damashq]]da Suriyadagi [[Shayzar]] hukmdori [[Usoma Ibn Munqiz|Usāma ibn Munqidh]] xizmatidagi ''[[Mamluklar|mamaliklar]]''ning nufuzli oilasida tug‘ilgan. Otasi 1169-yilda ham hayot bo‘lgan. Keyinchalik al-Shayzarī Yaman bo‘yicha [[Ayyubiylar]] noibi bo‘lgan [[Al-Muizz Ismail|al-Muʿizz Ismāʿīl ibn Ṭughtigīn]] ({{hukmronlik|1197|1201}}) saroyiga ko‘chib o‘tgan{{Sfn|Sellheim|1997}}. Unga yigirma besh bobdan iborat ''[[Odob|adab]]'' antologiyasi — ''ʿAjāʾib al-ashʿār wa-gharāʾib al-akhbār''ni bag‘ishlagan; undan faqat bitta o‘rta asr qo‘lyozmasi saqlangan bo‘lib, 1291-yil sanasi qo‘yilgan va hozir [[Peshovar]]da<ref>{{harvnb|Sellheim|1997}}; {{harvnb|King|1983|p=22}}; {{harvnb|Brockelmann|2017|loc=suppl. vol. 1, p. 467}}.</ref>. 1225-yilda u Ayyubiylar noibi [[al-Masʿūd Ṣalāḥ al-Dīn Yūsuf]] ({{hukmronlik|1215|1229}}) uchun o‘n olti kitobdan iborat yana bir antologiya — ''Jamharat al-Islām dhāt al-nathr wa ʾl-niẓām''ni tuzgan<ref>{{harvnb|Sellheim|1997}}; {{harvnb|Brockelmann|2017|loc=vol. 1, p. 262}}.</ref>. Unga o‘zi va o‘g‘li Ahmadning she’rlarini ham kiritgan{{Sfn|Sellheim|1997}}. ''Jamharat'' 1298-yil sanali yagona qo‘lyozmada saqlangan bo‘lib, hozir [[Leiden|Leyden]]da<ref>{{harvnb|Sellheim|1997}}, citing {{harvnb|Ahmad|1956}} and the facsimile edition of {{harvnb|Sezgin|1986}}.</ref>. Al-Masʿūdga bag‘ishlangan ikkinchi asar — ''ʿĀdāt al-nujūm'' bo‘lib, 1665 yoki 1666-yillarda ko‘chirilgan qo‘lyozma orqali ma’lum va hozir [[Sano|Ṣanʿāʾ]]da. U ''[[Kordova taqvimi]]''ga o‘xshash astronomik [[Almanax|almanax]]dir. U Yamanda foydalanish uchun tuzilmagan{{Sfn|King|1983|p=22}}.

Al-Shayzarīning vafot sanasi noma’lum{{Sfn|Sellheim|1997}}.
