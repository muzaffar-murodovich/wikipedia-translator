'''Sirāj al-Dīn Mahmūd ibn Abī Bakr Urmavī''' (also spelled '''Urmawī'''; 1198–1283) was a [[Shafiʽi school|Shafiʽi]] jurist, logician and philosopher from [[Urmia]] in [[Azerbaijan (Iran)|Azerbaijan]], a region in north-western [[Iran]].{{sfn|Marlow|2010|pages=279-280}} He spent most of his scholarly life in [[Ayyubid dynasty|Ayyubid]]-ruled [[Cairo]], and from 1257 in [[Sultanate of Rum|Seljuk]]-ruled [[Konya]]. The [[Iranian diaspora]] he was part of, proficient in Persian and Arabic, contributed majorly to the [[Islamization]] and [[Persianization]] of [[Anatolia]].{{sfn|Marlow|2010|pages=279-280, 305}} Most of his extant works were written in Arabic but there is also one known work in Persian.{{sfn|Marlow|2010|pages=282-283}} He was an acquaintance of [[Rumi]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite journal|last=Çağrıcı|first=Mustafa|date=2009|title=SİRÂCEDDİN el-URMEVÎ|url=https://cdn2.islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/dosya/37/C37012242.pdf|journal=DV Islam Ansiklopedisi|language=tr|volume=37|pages=262–264}}</ref>
