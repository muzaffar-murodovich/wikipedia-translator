'''Nasir al-Din Shahriyar''' was the [[Baduspanid]] ruler (''[[ustandar]]'') of [[Ruyan (district)|Rustamdar]] from 1317 to 1325. He was the brother and successor of [[Shams al-Muluk Muhammad]].{{sfn|Madelung|1988|pp=385–391}}

During his reign, Shahriyar cut all contact with the [[Mongol Empire|Mongol]] [[Ilkhanate]], and supported his brother-in-law, the [[Bavand dynasty|Bavandid]] ruler [[Shah-Kaykhusraw]] ({{reign|1310|1328}}) against the Mongol commander Mu'min and his son [[Kutlushah]], and the powerful Kiya Jalali family of [[Sari, Iran|Sari]]. Shahriyar was murdered in 1325 by his nephew Iskandar at the instigation of brother [[Taj al-Dawla Ziyar]], who took the throne.{{sfn|Madelung|1988|pp=385–391}}
